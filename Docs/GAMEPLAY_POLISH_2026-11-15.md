## Model and asset boundaries

- IDs, prerequisites, mission evaluators, save formats and one-time reward claims retain their existing authority. Removing a panel does not remove its underlying recorded data.
- Solar overview motion is authored, compressed visual playback so outer planets visibly move. It is not a new ephemeris, navigation calculation or scoring clock. Reduced-motion preference freezes that decorative motion; mission-time orbit sampling remains controlled by the engine.
- The new background is NASA Scientific Visualization Studio / Ernie Wright, **Deep Star Maps (2012)**: <https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/3895/>. The original 8192×4096 JPEG is packaged unchanged for offline use. SHA-256 and exact download are in `Tools/EmbeddedUI/eyes-textures/starfield-provenance.json`. The Reference media credits include this asset. Existing menu/atlas sky assets retain their separate credits.
